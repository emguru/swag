FAQ

Why isn't SWAG open source?

The scoring layer and semantic profile approach are the product. Source-visible licensing lets you inspect what you deploy while protecting commercial and redistribution rights.

How do you justify the price?

Rank-1 retrieval quality directly affects answer accuracy and downstream CRAG costs. On our benchmark corpus, SWAG improved Precision@1 by 25% relative. Your pilot quantifies the same delta on your documents.

Can we skip the pilot?

Yes, if you have a labeled evaluation set and existing RAG metrics. Most teams use the pilot for internal buy-in and tuning baseline.

Does SWAG replace our vector database or LLM?

No. SWAG is a reranking layer that sits between vector retrieval and generation — compatible with FAISS, Qdrant, Chroma, Pinecone, and existing CRAG pipelines.

What should we send to get a quote?

Company size, industry, deployment model (on-prem / cloud / SaaS), approximate corpus size, monthly query volume, and whether you need OEM or redistribution rights.
